## Top-Ups

**Q: Which chains are supported?**

USDT on three chains: TRC20, ERC20, and Polygon.

Use TRC20 day to day — the gas is lowest. For small top-ups, ERC20 gas can easily end up looking significant next to the amount you're sending, which isn't worth it.

**Q: What happens if I send on the wrong chain?**

You'll most likely lose it. This is the most common real loss in this whole category.

Two rules: **check the chain shown in the app before you transfer**, don't go from memory; and **send a small test amount first** — a few dollars to verify the path, then send the larger amount once it arrives.

**Q: Can I move money back on-chain?**

**No.** MothCard doesn't currently support crypto withdrawals. Funds that come in can be spent and can be transferred within the platform, but they can't be moved back on-chain.
